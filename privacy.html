Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version: this website doesn't use cookies, ads, or analytics, and it doesn't ask you for any information. We only learn something about you if you email us.

Who we are

This website, redanvildigital.com, is run by Red Anvil Digital Works, an independent software studio based in Florida, USA ("we," "us").

What this policy covers

This policy covers this website only. Our apps have their own privacy policies. Campaign Hall's privacy policy is inside the Campaign Hall app, and it explains what that app collects and why.

What this website collects

  • Nothing from you directly. There are no accounts, forms, cookies, ads, or tracking tools on this website.
  • Standard server logs. Our hosting provider, GitHub, receives the technical details that every website visit sends, such as your IP address, your browser type, and the page you asked for. GitHub uses these to deliver the site and keep it secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Email you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your personal information.
  • We don't show ads or build advertising profiles.
  • We don't add you to a mailing list because you emailed us.

Children

This website is for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Links to other sites

This website may link to other sites, such as Kickstarter. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you from your emails, or ask us to delete it. Write to legal@redanvildigital.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact

Privacy and legal requests: legal@redanvildigital.com
