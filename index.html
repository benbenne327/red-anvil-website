Flagship

Your game happens around a table. Your app should too.

The easy way to run and play tabletop RPGs at your table.

Campaign Hall is a web app for running tabletop roleplaying games in person. The GM runs the session from a phone, tablet, or laptop, and players follow along on their own phones. No downloads, nothing to install.

Closed beta for game masters · coming soon Kickstarter · early 2027
  • Your dice, first.Roll your real dice and type the number in. "Roll for me" is there if someone forgot their bag.
  • Fights that keep moving.Everyone's phone shows whose turn it is, what they can do, and what just happened.
  • A guided character builder.Each player builds their hero step by step on their own phone, and Campaign Hall does the math.
  • Meet the Bard, your guide at the table.He welcomes new players and shows them around.
  • AI helpers for GMs (adults 18+).Session plans and recaps drafted for you to approve. AI never rolls your dice and never decides the rules.

Early concept art. The final art will be made by professional artists, funded by our Kickstarter.

Free for players, always. Campaign Hall opens in the US first. Other countries follow after legal checks. It's coming to Kickstarter in early 2027. Backing it helps a one-person studio get Campaign Hall ready to open to everyone, and pays professional artists to make the final art. If it reaches its stretch goals, that includes a Family Safety Review of kids' seats.

The studio

Real experience. New possibilities.

Red Anvil Digital Works is a one-person software studio in Florida, built piece by piece with a lot of heart. I'm an HVAC technician turned app developer, and I bring the same care to software that I brought to every job: show up, find the real problem, and fix it right. Right now I build everything myself, but as the business grows, I plan to bring in more minds and more help to make this dream a reality.

That hands-on background shapes how I build: useful software, clear screens, and ideas worth developing, beyond any one industry.
