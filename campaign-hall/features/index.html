Campaign Hall features

The easy way to run and play tabletop RPGs at your table.

Here's what Campaign Hall does today. The GM uses a phone, tablet, or laptop, and players use their own phones. No downloads.

FightsDiceCharactersInviteGM toolsAI helpersComing

Fights

Fights run on everyone's phone.

Every player sees the turn order and knows when it's their move. On your turn, pick your action and send it. The GM approves each attack, and everyone sees what happened.

  • Action, bonus action, move, and reaction chips show what you've used.
  • Conditions and death saves track themselves.
  • Players see foes as Unhurt, Hurt, Bloodied, or Down, never their exact HP.

Dice

Your real dice come first.

Real dice stay on the table. When it's time to roll, type what your die shows. Campaign Hall adds your modifier, shows the GM the total, and doubles the dice on a crit.

Forgot your dice bag? "Roll for me" is right underneath. The app rolls once and records it.

Characters

A guided character builder.

New players don't need the rulebook open. The builder walks each player through their hero step by step on their own phone, and Campaign Hall does the math.

  • Class, background, species, ability scores, skills, and gear, one step at a time.
  • Each choice shows what it does before you pick it.
  • Ready today for one rules set. More game systems are on the way.

Invite

One link brings the party in.

The GM shares one invite link. Players tap it, sign in, and the invite code is already filled in. One more tap and they're in the campaign.

Joining doesn't share any of your characters. You choose which one to bring.

GM tools

Run the fight from your phone.

The GM sees everything in one place and decides every call.

  • Approve each attack before it lands.
  • Roll in the open, or in secret so players don't see it in the log.
  • Ask a player for a saving throw, set conditions, and change HP.
  • Hidden foes, exact enemy HP, and your notes are never sent to players.

AI helpers · adults 18+

AI does the GM's homework.

GMs have two jobs: run the game, and do the homework. Campaign Hall's AI helpers take the homework.

  • Planner drafts your session plan from your notes.
  • Chronicle drafts the recap after you play.
  • You approve or toss every draft. AI never rolls your dice and never makes the rules call.
