The easy way to run and play tabletop RPGs at your table.

Campaign Hall is a web app for tabletop roleplaying games played in person. The GM runs the session from a phone, tablet, or laptop, and every player follows along on their own phone. Nothing to install.

Why Campaign Hall

Your game happens around a table. Your app should too.

  • Fights run on everyone's phone.See whose turn it is and pick your action. The GM approves each attack. Conditions and death saves track themselves.
  • Your real dice come first.Type what your die shows and Campaign Hall does the math. Forgot your dice bag? "Roll for me" is right underneath.
  • AI does the GM's homework.It drafts your session plan from your notes, then the recap afterward. You approve or toss every draft. It never rolls your dice.

Kickstarter

Free for players, always.

Campaign Hall comes to Kickstarter in early 2027, from February 2 to March 4. Backing it pays for what a small app needs before it opens to everyone, including human artists to replace the concept art.

Before then, a closed beta opens for game masters. Campaign Hall launches in the US first, with other countries to follow.
